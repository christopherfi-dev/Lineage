# LINEAGE

A Grade 3 adaptation game. Children follow a lineage of small animals across generations
and watch whether an inherited variation helps or hurts them where they already live.

**Start with `LINEAGE_M2_CLASSROOM_SCOPE.md`. Do one step at a time.**
