*Updated 2026-09-27 for Part 4 (scope decision 61): bigger word lists (12 habitat words for each place, up to 4 words for each trait), fresh names every story, "Type your own" and "Use my name", and a small filter for typed names. The sections below are the current rules.*

## When a family is named

- **Right after the child's first tap**, before generation 1 starts. Time waits: the animals wander, the generation clock doesn't run.
- **The question:** "What will you call your family?" It has three tappable names, like "The Mossfoot family", each with a speaker, and two more ways, each with a speaker: "Type your own" and "Use my name" (below).
- **If no name is tapped within 20 seconds,** one of the three is picked at random, and the sheet says "We picked a name for you." with a speaker. It stays up for about 3 seconds, then the story starts.
- **The countdown waits** while a line is being read aloud or a creature card is open, as on the other panels, and while the child types.
- **Every new story asks again:** "Try another family in this world" and "New world".

## The naming rule

A name is one word made of two halves: a word for the family's **habitat** (list 1), then a word for a **trait** that stands out in the family (list 2). Examples: Moss + foot = "Mossfoot", Reed + tail = "Reedtail", Sand + runner = "Sandrunner".

1. **Habitat:** the habitat most of the family lives in when it is tapped.
2. **Traits:** for each of the ten traits, how far the family's average is from the whole world's average at that moment. The three traits furthest from average give the three names, one trait each.
3. **Words:** each name takes a different first half from the habitat's list, and one of its trait's words. Which ones is picked afresh for every story, with the browser's own random numbers, so a new story offers new names even for the same family.
4. **Two checks:** no name joins two of the same letter where the halves meet ("Leaffoot" is hard to read), and no name is on list 3.
5. **The random pick** at 20 seconds uses the browser's own random numbers, never the engine's.

## Typing a name (scope decision 61)

- **"Type your own":** "Type a name for your family." The child types a whole name: "Zoe" makes "the Zoe family".
- **"Use my name":** "Type your first name." The child's name is joined to the word for the trait that stands out most in the family (the first word of its list): "Mia" and "paddle" make "the Miapaddle family".
- **Letters only,** in any alphabet ("Zoë", "José"), 1 to 12 of them; anything else can't be typed. The first letter is made a capital and the rest small: "mia" is "Mia".
- **The sheet moves to the top** while the child types, clear of the iPad's keyboard. "OK" (or the keyboard's Done) uses the name, and "Back" returns to the three names.
- **A small filter:** a name that is one of a few rude or unkind words, or has one of a few rude words inside it, isn't used, and the sheet says "Let's try a different name." with a speaker. The lists are in `game/src/names.js` (a few words only as the whole name, because they hide inside real names: "Cassie", "Shelly", "Grape").
- **Nothing typed leaves the iPad:** no network call is made, and the name is kept only for the story.
