LINEAGE moments

Each link opens the game straight into one moment, in a real game state. The ones deep in a story take a few seconds.

Teacher: full-length storyThe whole 76 generations. A story is 50 by default.
  1. ArrivalThe opening, before the first tap.
  2. Naming the familyRight after the first tap: three names, and time waits.
  3. A generationMid-story, just before a generation passes.
  4. A variationOne newborn with a new trait, glowing.
  5. Following a traitA glowing newborn's card: “Follow animals with …”.
  6. FollowingYour line's animals with the trait light up, counted. They are your line now.
  7. The rising counterThe fast-forward while the trait's count in your line rises, the whole line in view.
  8. Back to real timeThe count reached 20: its last number, and the slow-down to real time.
  9. An arrow to a babyA glowing baby off the screen: an arrow at the edge points to it.
  10. Stay with them?A very small line: every glowing baby's card offers only “Keep looking”.
  11. Growing, and whyReal time after a follow: your line with the trait is growing, with the table's reason.
  12. Dying off, and whyYour animals with the trait are dying off, with the table's reason: each fades with a little light rising, the camera on it.
  13. A line dies outThe last animals of a followed line fade, the camera on them.
  14. Why did they die out?Right there: guess why, from three reasons.
  15. Back to your line“They didn't make it. Back to your line.” The line before is yours again.
  16. Your line and your relativesSince your last choice: your line and your relatives here, counts with bars.
  17. Another family's cardAn animal of another family: its family beside your line, and how it differs.
  18. One of your relativesThe rest of your line in its place, in a quiet colour since your follow: your relatives here beside your line.
  19. GrowingYour line clearly bigger than last generation.
  20. ShrinkingYour line clearly smaller than last generation.
  21. The backup choiceNothing followed for a while: two or three options.
  22. A predictionThe journal's question, right after the first follow.
  23. A prediction's resultThe next follow: your line since the last choice, and what you thought.
  24. Visiting the waterThe camera flies to the water's edge, and the narration sums it up. Its animals swim.
  25. Visiting the groundThe same for the open ground.
  26. The endingA surviving line's ending, its first step: what happened, with your line baby by baby.
  27. A creature cardAn animal with a new trait, its card open.
  28. About as well as your relativesA trait that doesn't matter here: your line did about as well as your relatives. Guess why.
  29. Away from your lineA glowing baby of your line living in another place: its card says so.
  30. Already chosenA glowing baby with the way back from a trait your line chose.
  31. Go back?Your line clearly died off the way it went: the way back is offered, with why.
  32. MovingSome of your line moves to another place, and the narration says so.
  33. Your line so farThe traits your line added up, as chips, one of them faded, with why.
  34. A reasonYour line changed size, and the narration says why, with “Helping here” (and “Hurting here” when something hurts).
  35. Why? (a result)Your line clearly growing after a follow: guess why, from three reasons.
  36. Why? (the answer)After the guess: why, from the table.
  37. Why? (a sudden drop)Your line shrank suddenly: guess why.
  38. Type your own nameNaming the family: typing a name, letters only.
  39. Use my name“Mia” makes the Miapaddle family.
  40. Your animals, on averageThe living portrait, opened: the average body and your line, baby by baby.
  41. Ending: your ideaThe sentence to build: why did your animals survive?
  42. Ending: check my ideaThe idea against the table, with the clue and your last choice's line beside its relatives.
  43. Ending: the revealThe real animal, the traits, the Field Guide and the story card.
  44. The story cardOne picture of the story, to save or share.
  45. A discoveryA follow's result went the table's way: “You discovered: …”.
  46. The Field GuideEach trait in each place: what you've discovered so far.
  47. Visiting the leavesIts animals on branches among the leaves.
  48. The mapZoomed out: the places' names and the borders between them.
