## What is on screen

Every moment is the same game state as its before shot (the same generation, family, animals and text). Only the look and the motion changed. Each picture is taken the same time after the moment opens as before, except for four shots:

| File | When | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `arrival-*.jpg` | 9 s after the page opens | The mist lifts over about 7 s; after it, the opening looks like the before shot. |
| `arrival-mist-*.jpg` | 3.8 s | New: the mist lifting as the camera drifts down into the leaves. |
| `generation-dawn-*.jpg` | 4.6 s | New: the next generation arrives at dawn (a generation is one day). `generation-*.jpg` is the end of the night before it. |
| `ending-*.jpg`, `extinct-*.jpg` | 4 s | The ending's parts come in one after another; the "Did you know?" notes last, at about 3.5 s. |
